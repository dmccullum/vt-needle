How this works

Each town starts from how it voted relative to the state in the 2016–2022 governor races. As towns report, the gap between actual and expected results gives a statewide swing and county swings, which are applied to the towns still out. Turnout is handled the same way. The chance of winning is the share of 2,000 simulations each candidate wins. The needles jitter within the model’s uncertainty.
